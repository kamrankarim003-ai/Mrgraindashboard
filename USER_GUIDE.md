# MR GRAIN Business Command Centre — Quick Start Guide

## Logging In

1. Go to the app's web address and enter your email and password.
2. Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen and follow the emailed link.
3. Once logged in, you'll land on the **Dashboard** — your daily overview.

## Adding a Customer

1. Click **Customers** in the left menu, then **+ Add Customer** (or use the same button on the Dashboard).
2. Fill in the business name, contact details, and pick a **Customer Type** and **Lead Source**.
3. Click **Save Customer**. You'll see them appear in the Customers list straight away.
4. Open a customer to log notes, set a follow-up date, or mark them **Won/Lost**.

## Creating an Order

1. From a customer's page, click **Create Order** (or use **Orders → + Add Order**).
2. Pick the customer, add one or more products with quantity in bags, and set a delivery date.
3. Click **Create Order**. New orders start as **Draft** — nothing leaves the warehouse yet.
4. Open the order and click **Move to confirmed** when it's ready. This is the point stock is deducted.
5. Keep moving it forward — **packed → dispatched → delivered → completed** — as it progresses.

## Generating an Invoice

1. Open a **confirmed** order (or later stage) and click **Generate Invoice**.
2. The invoice is created automatically from the order's items and totals, including GST if the business is GST-registered.
3. From the invoice page you can **Download PDF** or **Email to Customer**.

## Recording a Payment

1. Open the invoice and click **Record Payment**.
2. Enter the amount, date, and payment method, then save.
3. The invoice's balance and status (Partially Paid / Paid) update immediately, and a receipt PDF is available for download.

## Adding a Task

1. Click **Tasks → + Add Task**.
2. Give it a title, assign it to a team member, set a priority and due date.
3. Set **Recurrence** if it should repeat (daily/weekly/monthly) — completing a recurring task automatically creates the next one.
4. Tasks due today or overdue show up on the Dashboard and in the notification bell (top right).

## Everyday Tips

- **Status badges** are colour-coded everywhere: green = good, amber = pending/in progress, red = overdue or urgent.
- The bell icon (top right) shows notifications — overdue invoices, low stock, tasks due, and follow-ups.
- Each role only sees the menu items and buttons relevant to their job — this is normal and can't be changed by users themselves.
- Every list (Customers, Orders, Invoices, Expenses, Inventory, Reports) has an **Export** button for Excel.
